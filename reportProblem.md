# Reporting a problem
{: #report_problem}

## Community forums
{: #forums}

For issues related to lite or paid queue managers, there are several forums where you
can ask questions and read the responses to many existing queries.

* [MQSeries.net](http://www.mqseries.net/phpBB2/index.php){: external}
* [StackOverflow](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/ibm-mq){: external}
* [List-Server](https://listserv.meduniwien.ac.at/archives/mqser-l.html){: external}
* [IBM TechXchange](https://community.ibm.com/community/user/communities/globalgrouphome?CommunityKey=183ec850-4947-49c8-9a2e-8e7c7fc46c64){: external}

## Raising a support ticket
{: #support_ticket}

For issues related to a paid queue manager, please open a support ticket by accessing the **Support Center** from the help option in the IBM Cloud header bar.
